How DealHound checks a deal

Every price on this page was read from a named deals source or store page on 27 Nov 2026 — the source is printed on each card. The discount is always computed from the sale price and the usual price shown, so the numbers add up. Each deal carries a verdict: NEW LOW means the source called it the lowest price seen; STRONG means a big, genuine cut; ROUTINE means a small or ordinary sale. Store links go to the store's own page for that product. Prices move fast on Black Friday — check the store page before you buy.

DealHound is an independent deal tracker. Store names belong to their owners; links open the store in a new tab. Prices in US dollars unless a deal says per month.
